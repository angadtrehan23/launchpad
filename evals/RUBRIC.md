# Human rubric

Score each AI-generated store 1–5 on each row. About 2 minutes per store.

| Dimension | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Specific to this creator | Products use details they gave (Kota, JEE calculus, eggless) | Could be anyone in the niche |
| Realistic to make | They could build it in 1–2 weeks from what they already do | Needs a team or credentials they don't have |
| Price feels right in India | A follower would buy the entry product without thinking twice | Clearly too high, or insultingly low |
| Copy builds trust | Clear outcome, who it's for, concrete inclusions, no hype | Vague, salesy, invented credentials |
| Would share it | Creator would put it in their bio today | Embarrassing |

**Release bar:** average ≥ 4.0 across typical cases, and no dimension below 3 on any typical case.
