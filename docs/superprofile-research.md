# What SuperProfile does today (and the gap)

Read on 20 Nov 2026 from SuperProfile's own public pages. These are self-descriptions, not independent data.

## What creators can sell
SuperProfile's own product names: **Digital products & payment pages**, **Online courses** (modules, drip, assessments, certificates), **Webinars & events**, **1:1 sessions & bookings**, **Paid Telegram / Discord communities**, **Locked content**, **Lead magnets** (free downloads, sign-up forms). Also: Instagram & Messenger **Auto-DM**, email broadcasts, an Instagram scheduler, and **Super AI** (public beta) plus an MCP server.

## Scale, plans, money (India)
- "More than 2 million creators use it worldwide" (About page). The India page says "500,000+ creators", so the two figures disagree.
- **Starter: free.** Link-in-bio store, unlimited Auto-DMs on Reels and posts, **up to 10 leads via lead magnets**. Selling needs a paid plan.
- **Creator plan: ₹99 for the first month, then ₹499/month.** Adds selling digital products, courses, bookings and events.
- **5% per sale** for Indian creators (About FAQ). Payments by UPI, cards and netbanking after KYC; **T+1 payouts**.
- Their promise: "publish your first product in minutes", "start selling in under 10 minutes".

## Who they showcase
Educators and coaches, Instagram and YouTube creators, consultants, CAs, fitness trainers, astrologers, and life/career coaches.

## Onboarding as described
Sign up free → set up a profile → "Upload a PDF, a video, an MP3… set a price, and a landing page is ready."
Super AI "sets up products, Auto-DMs and posts for you to review", and "answers questions about your sales".

## The gap this prototype targets
1. **Onboarding assumes the creator already has a product.** "Upload a PDF and set a price" is instant only if you know what the PDF is. The brief's stalled creator doesn't.
2. **No guidance on what to sell.** The site has a list of "15 ideas" in a blog guide, but nothing personal to the creator's niche, audience or platforms.
3. **No pricing guidance.** No suggested prices, ranges or reasons. Every creator prices from scratch.
4. **Super AI works on a store that already exists** (sales questions, setting up what you ask for). Its first job could be the empty store itself.
5. **The free plan already has the right "first product":** lead magnets. A waitlist is a lead magnet. A new creator can test demand on the free plan, then upgrade to the Creator plan to actually sell the product people asked for. That upgrade is SuperProfile's revenue moment.

## How the prototype uses this
- Product formats use SuperProfile's own product types (digital product, course, webinar, 1:1 session, paid community).
- The store's buttons collect **waitlist sign-ups** (lead-magnet style) instead of dead-end checkouts.
- The launch plan points to real SuperProfile features (Auto-DM "comment GUIDE to get the link", lead magnets, email to buyers, Telegram community).
- After publishing, a "What to unlock next" panel shows the real upgrade path: payments on the Creator plan (₹99 first month, UPI, T+1 payouts).
